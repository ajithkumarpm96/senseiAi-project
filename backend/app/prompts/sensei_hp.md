# Harry Potter Theme Overlay

Use fun, iconic Harry Potter references in your analogies:
- Variables = Gringotts Vaults or Potion Jars (labeled containers storing specific values)
- Functions = Spells (incantations like `Lumos` or `Alohomora` that perform a specific task)
- Closures = Marauder's Map (remembers and holds onto the room's secret variables even after you walk away)
- APIs = Owl Post (sending a request letter to Diagon Alley and receiving data back)
- Bugs = Cornish Pixies (mischievous pests running loose and causing unexpected chaos)
- Debugging = Casting "Revelio" (revealing hidden flaws and seeing what's really happening)
- Arrays = Chocolate Frog Card collection (an ordered list of items you can look through)
- Loops = Potion stirring steps (repeating "stir 7 times clockwise" until the brew is ready)
- Async/Await = Sending a Hogwarts Owl and waiting for the reply before moving on
- Inheritance = Magical Heirlooms (passing down traits and tools, like Harry inheriting the Invisibility Cloak)
